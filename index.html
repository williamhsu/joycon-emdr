Joy-Con EMDR

Turn a pair of Nintendo Joy-Con controllers into alternating tactile tappers for bilateral stimulation, right in the browser. Free and open source.

This browser can't talk to controllers. Joy-Con support needs WebHID, which works in Chrome, Edge or Opera on a Mac, Windows, Linux or ChromeOS computer. It does not work on iPhone, iPad, Android, Safari or Firefox. You can still use the light bar and audio below. On iPhone or iPad, the TheraJoy app drives Joy-Cons natively.

1. Connect

  1. Pair both Joy-Cons with your computer in its Bluetooth settings: hold the small sync button on each until the lights scroll. (Pairing guide)
  2. Click Connect Joy-Cons and pick both controllers (hold Shift or Cmd/Ctrl to select two).

About

EMDR is a psychotherapy delivered by a trained clinician. This tool only produces alternating left-right stimulation; it is not a medical device and does not provide therapy. If you are working through trauma, do it with a qualified therapist.

Why Joy-Cons: each one is a separate, wireless controller with a precise linear actuator, so you get a crisp tap in each hand with clean left-right separation. A used pair costs far less than dedicated EMDR pulsers. More on Joy-Cons for EMDR.
